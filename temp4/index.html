Tips & Tricks

Level up how you learn

Quick, battle-tested strategies from our top educators to make every study session more effective.

01

Use Active Recall, Not Passive Re-reading

Close your notes and try to recall concepts from memory. This strengthens neural pathways far more effectively than re-reading the same page twice.

02

Space Your Revision with Intervals

Review content at 1, 3, 7, and 21 days after first learning it. Spaced repetition is proven to increase long-term retention by over 80%.

03

Teach It to Truly Understand It

The Feynman Technique: explain a concept out loud in simple words. Gaps in your explanation reveal exactly what you need to revisit.

04

Focus Blocks of 25 Min, Not Marathons

Use the Pomodoro Technique — 25 minutes of focused work followed by a 5-minute break. Prevents burnout and keeps concentration sharp.

05

Start with the Hardest Topic First

Your willpower peaks in the morning. Tackle the most difficult subject first and leave easier tasks for lower-energy periods of the day.

06

Mock Tests Beat Re-reading Old Notes

Solving past papers and mock tests reveals real gaps faster than revision. Time yourself strictly for authentic exam simulation.
