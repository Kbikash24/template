What exams does PrepLab cover?

PrepLab covers 80+ competitive exams including UPSC CSE, SSC CGL, IBPS PO, RRB NTPC, NEET, JEE, GATE, CAT, CLAT, NDA, and CDS. We continuously add new courses based on student demand.

Are courses self-paced or live?

We offer both! Self-paced recorded courses let you learn at your own schedule. Live batch programs run in real-time with interactive doubt sessions, mentorship, and peer collaboration.

How does the certification work?

After completing all modules and passing the final quiz (minimum 70%), you receive a digitally signed PrepLab certificate — downloadable as PDF and shareable on LinkedIn with a blockchain verification link.

Is there a refund policy?

Yes! We offer a 7-day no-questions-asked refund policy for all paid courses. Contact support within 7 days of purchase and we'll process your full refund within 3–5 business days.

Is content available in regional languages?

Yes! PrepLab supports Hindi, Bengali, Tamil, Telugu, Marathi, Gujarati, and English. Most courses have bilingual content, and our AI assistant is fully multilingual.

Can I access courses on mobile?

Absolutely. PrepLab works seamlessly on Android, iOS, tablets, and desktops. Download lessons for offline access anytime, anywhere.
